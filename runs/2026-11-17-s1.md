**Found on the way.**

- The S0 spike's icon was never visible. The widget had no implicit size, so
  its button had none and the glyph sat half above the screen. Omarchy's
  panels take `implicitWidth`/`implicitHeight` from their button; this one
  now does.
- QML's JavaScript takes no `\u{f0849}` escape. The glyph is the surrogate
  pair `󰡉`.
- A glyph inside `iconComponent` is placed by the bar's content-bounds
  measure, which drew it off centre. The state is drawn with the button's own
  `text`, `foreground` and `active` instead: the group glyph lit or dimmed,
  its outline (`󰭘`) when svrngs is not installed, and the bar's
  urgent colour when an answer could not be read.
- A plugin linked into `~/.config/omarchy/plugins` does not reload on save:
  the shell's `inotifywait -r` does not follow the link, and
  `omarchy-shell shell rescanPlugins` rebuilt the widget from its cached
  component. `omarchy restart shell` loads the new code.
- svrngs updated itself at 20:40:58 during S0 (e) (exit 75, `home.rs:362`,
  the binary replaced); garden's lend answered Fedora again 12 s later.
